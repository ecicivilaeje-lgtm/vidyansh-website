The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur, has released Advertisement No. 10/2026 for the Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026. It covers Junior Engineer posts in three Rajasthan government departments: Public Works Department (PWD), Water Resources Department and Public Health Engineering Department (PHED). The posts are at Pay Matrix Level 10 of the state's 7th Pay Commission.

Applications ran from 16 August to 14 September 2026, and the exam is expected in February 2027. If you applied, the next step is preparing for the written exam. If you didn't, this guide shows what the 2026 cycle looked like, which helps you plan for the next one.

All posts also require working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture. Candidates in the final year of the qualifying course could apply, but they must obtain the qualification on or before the exam date, or they won't be eligible.

Age limit

Candidates must have completed 18 years and not turned 40 on 1 January 2027. The Board last counted age on 1 January 2025 for the earlier JE recruitment, so every applicant gets an extra 1 year of relaxation in the upper limit.

The relaxations in the first group are not cumulative: you get the benefit of only one of them. The 5-year relaxation for benchmark disabilities is the exception, because it is added to your category relaxation.

Nationality and other conditions

  • You must be a citizen of India, or fall in one of the other categories listed in the notice (Nepal, Bhutan, Tibetan refugees or persons of Indian origin from specified countries, who need a certificate of eligibility).
  • A male candidate with more than one living wife is not eligible. A woman who has married such a man is also not eligible.
  • Candidates with more than two children born on or after 1 June 2002 are not eligible, subject to the exceptions in the notice.
  • Candidates who took dowry at marriage are not eligible.
  • You must be medically fit and of good character. A good-character certificate and police verification are needed after selection.

Can you apply for more than one post?

Yes, if you meet each post's qualification. You must submit a separate application for each post category.

Reservation

  • Vertical: SC, ST, OBC, MBC (5%) and EWS (10%), applicable to Rajasthan domicile candidates only.
  • Women: 30% horizontal reservation, category-wise. Within it, 8% is for widows and 2% for divorced or abandoned women.
  • Ex-servicemen: 12.5% horizontal reservation. Dependants of ex-servicemen don't get this benefit.
  • Outstanding sportspersons: 2% horizontal reservation, for candidates with the specified sports certificates.
  • Persons with disabilities: 4% horizontal reservation in the PWD and PHED posts, for the disability categories listed in the notice. Candidates need a permanent disability certificate (40% or more) and a UDID card.

The category in your application decides the benefit you get, and it cannot be changed after the correction period.

Exam pattern

The selection is through a single written objective paper. The scheme is the same for all three posts.

PartSubjectMarks
Part AGeneral Knowledge: Rajasthan history, art and culture, literature, traditions and heritage; Rajasthan geography; political and administrative system of Rajasthan40
Part BTechnical subject: Civil Engineering (Degree), Civil Engineering (Diploma), or Mechanical/Electrical Engineering (Diploma)80
Total120
  • Duration: 2 hours, plus 10 extra minutes to check that every question is marked.
  • Negative marking: one-third of the marks of a question for every wrong answer.
  • Minimum qualifying marks: 40%. A candidate scoring less is not eligible for appointment.
  • Five options: each question has options A to E. Fill bubble E if you don't want to attempt a question. If you leave all five blank, one-third of the marks are deducted, for up to 10% of the questions. Leaving more than 10% of questions with no bubble filled disqualifies you.

Syllabus highlights

Part A (all posts): Rajasthan's history and major dynasties, Mughal-Rajput relations, architecture, forts and monuments, religious movements and folk deities, painting and handicrafts, language and literature, fairs and festivals, the princely states and 1857, peasant and tribal movements, and the integration of Rajasthan. Geography covers physical divisions, drainage, climate, soils, vegetation, wildlife, desertification, crops, livestock, irrigation, power and mineral resources. Polity covers urban local self-government, the 74th Amendment, Governor, Assembly, Chief Minister, Lokayukta, the State Human Rights, Information and Election Commissions, and the Rajasthan Public Services Guarantee Act, 2011.

Part B, JE (Civil) Degree: building technology and construction management, fluid mechanics, surveying, estimating and costing, irrigation and water resources, theory of structures and strength of materials, structural analysis, soil mechanics and foundation engineering, design of RC concrete and masonry structures, design of steel structures, construction technology, AutoCAD civil drawing, public health engineering, and highways and bridges.

Part B, JE (Civil) Diploma: building technology and construction management, surveying, estimating and costing, strength of materials, reinforced concrete design, irrigation and water resources, soil engineering and AutoCAD civil engineering drawing.

Part B, JE (Electrical) Diploma: basic concepts and circuit laws, magnetic circuits, AC fundamentals, measurement and instruments, electrical machines, synchronous machines, generation, transmission and distribution, estimation and costing, utilisation of electrical energy and basic electronics.

Part B, JE (Mechanical) Diploma: fluid mechanics and machines, internal combustion engines, strength of materials, theory of machines, AutoCAD mechanical drawing, thermodynamics and machine drawings.

How to apply

  1. Log in to the SSO portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in) with your SSO ID, open Citizen Apps (G2C), choose Recruitment Portal and click Apply Now.
  2. If you haven't done OTR (One Time Registration), complete it first and pay the fee. Enter your category, disability status and home state carefully, because the options available in the application depend on what you filled in OTR.
  3. Upload your latest photograph (taken within the last month), signature and thumb impression through OTR. The application picks these up automatically.
  4. Enter your visible body mark and select your educational qualification in the OTR dashboard. Documents go in the Document Details section.
  5. Choose your department preferences in the application, in the order you want them. This cannot be changed later.
  6. Click Final Submit. An application number is generated only after final submission, and a preview doesn't count as submitting. Keep a printout.

Use only your own mobile number, email and SSO ID. An application filled through someone else's SSO ID is not accepted, and details you entered in OTR can't be edited in the application. You can also fill the form through an authorised e-Mitra kiosk or CSC.

Documents for verification

  • Educational certificates and mark sheets for the qualification you claimed
  • Age proof and Rajasthan domicile certificate
  • Caste certificate (SC, ST, OBC, MBC) or EWS income and asset certificate, issued on or before the last date of application and in the candidate's own or father's name (a husband's name is not accepted for married women's category certificates)
  • Disability, ex-servicemen, sports, widow or divorce documents, where applicable
  • NOC from the present employer, for those already in government service
  • Marriage registration certificate and a declaration on the number of children, for married candidates

Selection and post allocation

  1. Appear in the written exam and score at least 40%.
  2. The Board prepares the merit list by category.
  3. Departments are allotted by Merit cum Priority, based on your rank and the department preferences you gave in the application.
  4. The departments verify documents, and you must pass the medical fitness test and character verification.

Selected candidates are appointed on a probation of two years. During this period they get a fixed monthly remuneration set by the state government, and no other allowances. After successful probation and confirmation, they move to the minimum of the pay level with allowances.

Preparation tips

  • Don't ignore Rajasthan GK. It is 40 of the 120 marks and is the easiest part to score in, if you read from a Rajasthan-specific source.
  • Prepare your technical subject in depth. It carries 80 marks, and for each post the subjects are listed topic by topic in the notice.
  • Practise OMR-style marking. The five-option format, with E for skipped questions, can cost you marks if you forget it.
  • Plan your department order early. Think about work profile, location and future postings before you fill preferences, because they are final.
  • Watch the negative marking. One-third of a mark is lost per wrong answer, so don't guess blindly.

Common questions

How many vacancies are there in RSSB JE 2026?

The notice lists 874 posts: 793 in the Non-Scheduled Area and 81 in the Scheduled Area, across PWD, Water Resources and Public Health Engineering departments.

Can I apply for more than one JE post?

Yes, but you must submit a separate application for each post category, such as JE (Civil) Degree, JE (Civil) Diploma and JE (Mechanical/Electrical) Diploma, if you are eligible for each.

Is there negative marking in the RSSB JE exam?

Yes. One-third of the marks of a question is deducted for every wrong answer. The minimum qualifying mark is 40 percent.

What is the age limit for RSSB JE 2026?

Candidates must have completed 18 years and not turned 40 on 1 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories, women, ex-servicemen and persons with disabilities.

Is knowledge of Hindi required?

Yes. The notice requires working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

Final word

RSSB JE 2026 offers 874 Junior Engineer posts in Rajasthan's PWD, Water Resources and PHED, for both degree and diploma holders in Civil, plus Mechanical/Electrical diploma holders.
